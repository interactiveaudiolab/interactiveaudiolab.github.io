Edilfanta (EJ) Van de Grift is a current BA/MS student at Northwestern University graduating in Spring (BA) and Fall 2027 (MS). His work in the Interactive Audio Lab primarily focuses on harmonic classification models and creating automated systems to mitigate the expensive nature of training them. He also explores real-time generative systems and finding efficient methods for training models by utilizing low-level optimizations. In his free time, he enjoys playing fingerstyle guitar, reading narrative non-fiction, and is (trying) to learn how to cook!
